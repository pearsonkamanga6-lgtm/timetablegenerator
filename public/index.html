What the generator enforces

No teacher clash; no class clash; teacher unavailable periods; exact weekly period totals; requested double sessions; doubles do not cross the main break; and the same class/group/subject is spread across days with a daily meeting limit.

Version 3 independently audits results. If full generation fails within the search limit, valid partial results and missing periods are reported.
