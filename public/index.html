How this version works

Enter or check your classes, teachers and subjects. Then use Teaching Load to say who teaches what and how many periods per week. Mark teacher unavailable times under Availability. Finally press Generate. The program checks obvious impossible loads before trying to build the timetable.

Your Term 3 project has been used as the starting point. The new TIMETABLE TERM3(1).roz copy contains a readable lesson-reference table. Version 2 has imported 167 source lesson records and merged them into 114 teaching-load rows linked to the real classes/groups, subjects and teachers. Imported block patterns are shown for checking; double-period rules are deliberately left at zero until you confirm them, so the app does not invent constraints.

Keep compulsory rules separate from preferences. Teacher unavailability and clashes are compulsory. Things such as “prefer Mathematics in the morning” can later be added as preferences without making generation unnecessarily impossible.

What the generator enforces

No teacher clash; no class clash; teacher unavailable periods; exact weekly period totals; requested double sessions; doubles do not cross the main break; and the same allocation is spread across days where possible.

Version 2.1 also preserves the deepest valid partial result when the complete problem cannot be solved within the search limit. That partial result can be viewed and exported for diagnosis.
